Berry Flames has entered the 2.0 state! Here's what's changed:

**Backend**
- Berry moved to a proper OOP structure!
- Source code now up on github, and shared with the owner!

**Banking**
- Added the `/get_bits` command! No need to chat with Berry to get your current balance!
- Berry now rewards bits for chatting in public and custom chat rooms!
- Sending bits is more secure (oops)!

**Chatting**
- Berry is less chatty! You have to say her name to chat with her!
- Added `/pie_attack`! Berry will now surprise your target with a pie after a random amount of messages! 25 bits for general chat, 100 for everywhere else!

**Private room creation**
- You no longer need to specify yourself as the participant when using `/create_room`. Berry now assumes you are meant to participate!
- Added the `/toggle_room_visibility` command! You can now flaunt your roleplay skills to all members of our community!
- Berry no longer announces your private room's creation (stay safe, lover pones!)

**Help**
- There's a new, context dependant `/help` command! It **should** make a hint for all available slash commands, depending on what channel you used it in!

**Quick data privacy QnA**
I was asked what berry tracks exactly, so for transparency, here's a quick QnA ^^
Q: Does Berry track my messages?
A: Not quite. Berry gives a single "bit" for every message sent, but that's it.

Q: Does Berry store the contents of my messages?
A: No.

Q: Does Berry read the contents of my messages?
A: Yes. Berry looks for keywords that would trigger certain replies! But she never reads (or cares for) the content/meaning of the message.
